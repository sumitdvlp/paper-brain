# WMT 2014

## What it is

The 2014 Workshop on Machine Translation shared-task data. Papers in this vault
use two pairs: English-German (about 4.5M sentence pairs) and English-French
(about 36M sentences), reporting BLEU on the newstest2014 test set
([[attention-is-all-you-need|Vaswani 2017]]).

## Key facts

- Metric: BLEU, higher is better.
- Caveat _(background, not from a paper in this vault)_: BLEU depends on
  tokenisation and scoring script, so scores from different papers are only
  comparable when both say how they computed it.

## Why it appears here

The headline benchmark for [[transformer]] translation results.
